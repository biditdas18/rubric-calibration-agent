# Human validation data

## `human_validation_all_rounds.csv` — consolidated, use this one

Every human rating collected for CRUCIBLE, both rounds, one row per
(annotator, item) pair. 209 rows.

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `domain` | `general_education`, `tech_ai`, or `career_selfimprovement` |
| `round` | `1` (even global transcript indices) or `2` (odd) |
| `transcript_index` | Global 0-based row index into `data/review_queue.csv`; blank for attention checks |
| `item_id` | Platform item identifier as shown to annotators |
| `is_attention_check` | `True` for the one synthetic screener item per batch — not corpus material |
| `annotator_id` | Pseudonym (see below) |
| `included_in_consensus` | `False` for the one round-1 Technology & AI annotator who was rejected and replaced; their ratings are retained for the rejection-sensitivity analysis |
| `label` | `HIGH` or `LOW` |
| `confidence` | Self-reported 1–5 |
| `justification` | Free-text reason written by the annotator |

Filtering to `is_attention_check == False and included_in_consensus == True`
gives exactly 180 rows: 60 transcripts × 3 raters.

### Design

20 transcripts per domain — the whole corpus — scored in two rounds of 10.
Each round recruited three annotators per domain (18 in total) via Prolific
with a >95% approval-rating filter. **The two rounds drew disjoint cohorts:**
no annotator appears in more than one round or more than one domain. Round 1
covers each domain's even global indices, round 2 the odd ones.

Because the cohorts are disjoint, a single pooled agreement statistic mixes
within-cohort and between-cohort variation. Report per-round values alongside
any pooled figure; `results/human_validation_n20.json` does both.

### Annotator pseudonyms

`annotator_id` is `{DOMAIN}_A{n}` for round 1 and `{DOMAIN}_R2_A{n}` for
round 2, with `DOMAIN` in `GENED` / `TECHAI` / `CAREER`. **Raw platform
participant identifiers are deliberately not present in this repository**, in
any file or any revision. The pseudonyms are stable within this dataset and
carry no information beyond domain and round; they are not linkable to
platform accounts.

### Attention checks

One synthetic item per batch, carried forward verbatim between rounds. Do not
use them as a quality filter: scoring round-1 annotators' actual responses
against the intended keys flags 2 of 9 demonstrably attentive annotators, and
in round-2 Career the single annotator who "failed" produced the most
discriminating label vector in the study while an annotator who used one label
throughout passed automatically. Two of the three keys are set to the domain's
*majority* class, so they cannot fail an inattentive rater — only a
discriminating one. Treat them as a diagnostic covariate, never as a rejection
criterion. The `justification` free text is the load-bearing quality signal.

## Other files

- `human_validation_ground_truth_n20.csv` — per-item majority-vote consensus
  for all 60 transcripts, with the system label and match flag. Regenerated by
  `src/agents/exp_b14_human_validation_n20.py`.
- `human_validation_ground_truth.csv` — the original round-1-only consensus
  (n=30). Kept unchanged so earlier analyses that reference it still run.
- `CRUCIBLE Human Validation Study — 2026 (*)/` — raw per-batch platform
  exports for round 1. The Technology & AI directory holds both `v1(reject)`
  and `v2`; `v2` is canonical, and its official trio is Annotator2/3/4.
